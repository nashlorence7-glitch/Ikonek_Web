FAQ

Questions, answered.

COMPATIBILITY

iPhone

Works automatically on iPhone XS and newer. Just hold the card near the top of the phone. On iPhone 7, 8 and X, open Control Centre and tap the NFC tag reader first.

Android

Works on most phones with NFC. Check that NFC is on in Settings, unlock the screen, then touch the card to the back of the phone.

No NFC? No problem

Black and White cards carry a QR code on the back as backup. Any phone camera can scan it. No app needed.

Google Review Card

No NFC on their phone? It comes with a sticker for your store, and we provide a QR code that opens the same Google review page. Customers just scan it with their camera. No app needed.

GOOD TO KNOW

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, on both. iPhone XS and newer read the card automatically when you hold it near the top of the phone. Most Android phones with NFC do the same, as long as NFC is switched on in Settings. Black and White cards also have a QR code on the back, so phones without NFC can scan that instead.

Does the other person need an app?

No. Neither of you needs an app. They hold their phone to your card, or scan the QR code, and your profile opens in their normal browser.

What if the card does not scan?

Unlock the phone and hold the card flat against the back of it, near the top where the NFC antenna sits. Take off any thick or metal case if it still does not read. You can always show the QR code on the back of your Black or White card, or send your profile link directly.

What happens if I lose my card?

The card only holds a link to your profile page, so it does not store your private details. Message us as soon as you notice and we will help you get a replacement card that opens the same profile. Your page, details and links stay exactly as they are.

Can I change my details after I get the card?

Yes. The card opens your digital profile, so your phone number, links, photo or services can be updated without printing a new card. Message us what you want changed and your profile is updated. The same card keeps working and the new details show the next time someone taps it.

Can you design the card and page to match my brand?

Yes. Send us your logo and colors and we design both the physical card and the profile page around them.

Can you build a website for my business?

Yes. We design and build a mobile-friendly website for your shop or business, with your products or services, photos, contact details and Messenger or Facebook links. Features and pricing are tailored to your needs, so request a quotation and tell us what you have in mind.

What if my customer's phone doesn't have NFC? (Google Review Card)

No worries. Your Google Review Card comes with a sticker you can put anywhere in your store, such as the counter, door, window or a table, and we provide a QR code that opens the same Google review page. Customers scan it with their regular phone camera and the review page opens right away. No app needed.

How long does an order take?

Message us with what you need and we will confirm timing and price.
